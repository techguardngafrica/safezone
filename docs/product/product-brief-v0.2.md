## 1. Product Overview

Safezone is a multi-tenant estate management and smart community platform designed to digitize and centralize the administration, security, financial operations and everyday activities of residential estates.

The platform connects estate owners, estate managers, residents, security personnel, service providers, advertisers and platform administrators through one integrated digital ecosystem.

Safezone provides tools for:

- Estate and property management.
- Resident and tenancy management.
- Visitor management and access control.
- Security operations and emergency response.
- SOS and emergency contact management.
- Estate billing and financial management.
- Electricity, water and utility payments.
- Maintenance and complaint management.
- Community communication and engagement.
- Facility and asset management.
- Vendor services and marketplace.
- Procurement and inventory.
- Smart estate and IoT integrations.
- Paid advertising and sponsored placements.
- Platform-wide administration and analytics.

## 2. Product Vision

To become a leading digital platform for managing residential estates and connected communities, making estates safer, smarter, more organized and easier to manage.

## 3. Problem Statement

Residential estates frequently depend on fragmented systems and manual processes for managing residents, security, finances, maintenance and communication.

Safezone addresses these problems by providing a centralized digital platform that improves operational efficiency, transparency, accountability, security and resident experience.

## 4. Target Users and Stakeholders

1. **Estate Owners:** Individuals or organizations that own or develop residential estates.
2. **Estate Managers:** Personnel responsible for managing estate operations.
3. **Property Owners:** Individuals who own properties within estates.
4. **Residents:** Homeowners, tenants and authorized household members.
5. **Security Personnel:** Security guards, supervisors and patrol officers.
6. **Estate Employees:** Accountants, facility managers and maintenance personnel.
7. **Service Providers:** Verified businesses providing services to estates and residents.
8. **Advertisers:** Businesses, individuals, vendors, estates and organizations interested in purchasing advertising placements.
9. **Safezone Administrators:** Techguard personnel responsible for managing the platform.

A person may hold multiple roles. For example, an estate manager may also be a homeowner and resident.

## 7. Community Communication

Safezone will include a dedicated community communication system that supports multiple levels of interaction.

### Building-Level Communication

Residents occupying units within the same building can communicate with one another.

Examples:

- Building maintenance discussions.
- Water supply issues.
- Shared facility concerns.
- Building-specific announcements.

### Estate-Wide Community Channels

Residents across the estate can participate in organized discussions.

Examples:

- General community discussions.
- Security updates.
- Events and activities.
- Management announcements.
- Community suggestions.

### Resident-Created Channels

Residents can create interest-based channels, subject to estate policies and moderation rules.

### Private Messaging

Residents should be able to communicate privately where permitted by estate configuration.

Estate management must have moderation capabilities, including reporting, muting, channel restrictions and content removal.

Safezone will include an advertising platform that allows external and internal stakeholders to purchase promotional placements within the application ecosystem.

Potential advertisers include:

- Businesses.
- Service providers.
- Marketplace vendors.
- Other residential estates.
- Residents and individual users.
- Property developers.
- Educational institutions.
- Financial service providers.
- External organizations and brands.

Advertising will be a separate commercial service managed by Techguard.

### Advertising Capabilities

- Advertising enquiries.
- Advertiser registration.
- Advertiser verification.
- Campaign creation.
- Creative submission.
- Advertisement approval.
- Placement selection.
- Campaign scheduling.
- Target audience configuration.
- Advertising payments.
- Campaign activation and suspension.
- Impression tracking.
- Click tracking.
- Conversion tracking where supported.
- Advertising performance reports.
- Campaign renewal.

### Advertising Placements

Potential placements include:

- Resident application home screen.
- Community feed.
- Community channel sponsored posts.
- Marketplace promotional sections.
- Utility and service discovery screens.
- Relevant announcement and discovery areas.
- Other approved application placements.

All paid content must be clearly identified as Sponsored or Advertisement.

Advertising must not interfere with SOS, security operations, visitor verification, financial transaction confirmation or other critical user workflows.
